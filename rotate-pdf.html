Rotate PDF

Fix sideways or upside-down pages.

How to use Rotate PDF

  1. Tap Choose a PDF file and pick your document.
  2. Choose the angle: 90 degrees clockwise, 180 degrees or 90 degrees counter-clockwise.
  3. Leave the pages box empty to rotate every page, or type pages like 1-3, 5 to rotate only those.
  4. Tap Rotate pages, then download the fixed PDF.

About this tool

Rotate PDF fixes pages that come out sideways or upside down. This happens often with phone scans and with documents saved from photo apps.

Pick the direction and rotate the whole file or only some pages. The rotation is saved inside the new PDF, so it opens the right way in every PDF viewer and on every device.

Rotating does not change the quality of your pages, and text stays selectable.

Your file stays on your device. It is processed inside your browser and is never uploaded to a server.

Questions

Can I rotate only one page?

Yes. Type that page number in the pages box, for example 3, and only that page is rotated.

Does rotating reduce the quality?

No. The pages are not redrawn. Only the page direction is changed.

Which direction is clockwise?

Clockwise turns the top of the page towards the right, like the hands of a clock.

Can I rotate a password-protected PDF?

Not yet. Remove the password first, then try again.
