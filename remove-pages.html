Remove pages

Delete the pages you do not want and keep the rest.

How to use Remove pages

  1. Tap Choose a PDF file and pick your document.
  2. Type the pages you want to delete, for example 2, 4-6.
  3. Tap Remove pages.
  4. Tap Download to save the cleaned PDF.

About this tool

Remove pages deletes the pages you do not need. Common examples are blank pages from a scanner, a cover sheet, or extra pages you do not want to print or upload.

Type the page numbers to delete, using commas for single pages and dashes for ranges. The remaining pages keep their order and their quality, and the text stays selectable.

Your original file is not touched. If you make a mistake, just run the tool again with the right page numbers.

Your file stays on your device. It is processed inside your browser and is never uploaded to a server.

Questions

Can I undo a removal?

Your original file is never changed, so you can start again from it. Only the new file has the pages removed.

Can I remove every page?

No. At least one page must stay in the file.

How do I write the page numbers?

Separate pages with commas and use a dash for a range. For example, 2, 4-6 removes pages 2, 4, 5 and 6.

Will removing pages make the file smaller?

Usually yes, but the size depends on what the removed pages contained. For a much smaller file, use Compress PDF.
