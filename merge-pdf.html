Merge PDF

Combine several PDFs into one file, in the order you choose.

How to use Merge PDF

  1. Tap Choose PDF files and select two or more PDFs. You can tap again to add more.
  2. Use the up and down arrows to put the files in the order you want. Tap the cross to remove a file.
  3. Tap Merge PDFs.
  4. Tap Download merged.pdf to save your new file.

About this tool

Merge PDF combines two or more PDF files into one document. Students use it to join assignment pages, job seekers use it to put a resume, certificates and ID proofs into a single file, and offices use it to bundle invoices and reports.

Add your files, arrange them with the up and down arrows and tap Merge PDFs. Pages are copied exactly as they are, so text stays sharp and nothing is turned into a picture.

If the final file is too large to upload somewhere, run it through Compress PDF afterwards. If some of your pages are photos, turn them into a PDF with Image to PDF first and then merge everything together.

Your file stays on your device. It is processed inside your browser and is never uploaded to a server.

Questions

How many PDFs can I merge at once?

There is no fixed limit. It depends on your device memory, so very large files may be slow on an older phone. If that happens, merge a few files at a time.

Does merging reduce the quality?

No. Pages are copied as they are, so the quality and the selectable text stay the same.

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

Not yet. Remove the password first, then add the file again.

Can I merge photos and PDFs together?

Yes, in two steps. Convert the photos with Image to PDF, then merge that PDF with your other files.
