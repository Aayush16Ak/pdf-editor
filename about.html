PDF Editor

About PDF Editor

PDF Editor is a small, independent project that offers free tools for everyday PDF work. You can edit a PDF by adding text, cover or highlight, merge and split files, remove or rotate pages, compress a large file, add page numbers or a watermark, and convert between PDF, images and text.

Why we built it

Many PDF websites ask you to upload your documents to a server, create an account or pay to remove limits. We wanted tools that are quick to open, free to use and careful with your files, especially for students and people filling forms on a phone.

How your files are handled

Every tool runs inside your own browser. Your PDFs and images are read by your device, changed by your device and saved by your device. They are not sent to our servers, because we do not run a server that receives files.

Keeping the site free

The tools are free to use. To cover the cost of running the site, we may show advertising in the future. Ads will never change how your files are handled.
