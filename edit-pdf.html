Edit PDF

Add text, cover mistakes with a white box and highlight parts of your PDF.

How to use Edit PDF

  1. Tap Choose a PDF file and pick the document you want to edit.
  2. Pick a tool: Add text, Cover or Highlight. Add text works with a tap, while Cover and Highlight work by dragging over the page.
  3. Use A- and A+ to change the text size, choose a colour, and use Move to drag items into the exact place. Zoom in on a phone for small print.
  4. Tap Save PDF, then Download. Your original file is not changed.

About this tool

Edit PDF lets you write on top of a PDF page without installing any app. Add your name, a date or a short answer to a form. Cover a small mistake with a white box and type the correct text over it. Highlight important lines in notes or study material before you share them.

PDF files do not store text like a Word document, so the words already inside the file cannot be changed directly. That is why this tool works in layers. You place new text, white boxes and highlights on top of the page, and when you save, they become a permanent part of the new PDF. This works well for forms, applications, names, dates and small corrections on documents with a plain white background.

Text in Hindi and other languages is supported too. If a language is not covered by the standard PDF fonts, the text is saved as a small picture so that it still looks right, although it can no longer be selected.

Your file stays on your device. It is processed inside your browser and is never uploaded to a server.

Questions

Can I change the text that is already in my PDF?

Not directly. Cover the old text with a white box, then add the new text on top. This looks clean on most documents that have a plain white background.

Does Edit PDF work on a phone?

Yes. Use the zoom buttons to enlarge the page, then tap exactly where you want to type. Switch to Move when you want to scroll around or drag an item.

Can I edit a password-protected PDF?

Not yet. Remove the password in the app where you got the file, then try again.

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens in your browser, so your document never leaves your device.
