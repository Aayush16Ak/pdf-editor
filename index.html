Free PDF tools that respect your privacy

PDF Editor is a set of simple tools for everyday documents. Merge assignments, split a big file, compress a scan for an upload limit, turn photos into a PDF or add your name to a form. Pick a tool above and it opens right away, with no account to create.

Most online PDF sites upload your file to their server. Here, the work happens inside your browser instead, which is faster for small files and safer for documents such as ID forms and marksheets.

Questions

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Every tool works on your own device, using your browser. Your PDFs and images are never sent to a server.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no daily limit and no watermark added to your files.

Can I change the existing text in a PDF?

Not directly, because PDF text is not stored like a Word document. In Edit PDF you cover the old text with a white box and type the new text on top. This works well for forms, names, dates and small corrections.

Why can I not select text after compressing?

Compress turns each page into a smaller picture, which is what makes the file light. Use it for scanned notes, forms and photos.

Can I open a password-protected PDF?

Not yet. Remove the password in the app where you got the file, then try again.
