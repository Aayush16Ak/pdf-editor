Questions

Can I change the existing text in a PDF?

Not directly, because PDF text is not stored like a Word document. In Edit PDF you cover the old text with a white box and type the new text on top. This works well for forms, names, dates and small corrections.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Every tool works on your own device, using your browser. Your PDFs and images are never sent to a server.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no daily limit and no watermark added to your files.

Why can't I select text after compressing?

Compress turns each page into a smaller picture, which is what makes the file light. Use it for scanned notes, forms and photos. For PDFs where you need to copy text, use another tool instead.

Can I open a password-protected PDF?

Not yet. Remove the password in the app where you got the file, then try again.
