Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

This policy explains what information PDF Editor ("we", "us", "the site") handles when you use it.

Your files

All PDF tools on this site work inside your web browser. The PDFs, images and text you choose are processed on your own device and are not uploaded to or stored on our servers. We cannot see your files.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not ask for your name, phone number or address to use the tools. Like most websites, the company that hosts the site may keep basic technical logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and to keep the service running.

Cookies and advertising

We may show advertisements from third-party networks such as Google AdSense. These partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google uses advertising cookies to serve ads based on a person's visit to this site and other sites on the internet. You can manage personalised advertising in your Google Ads Settings at adssettings.google.com.

Third-party services

To work, the site loads software libraries and fonts from public content delivery networks such as cdnjs and Google Fonts. These services may receive your IP address when your browser requests those files.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please use the details on our Contact page.
