Add watermark

Stamp text like DRAFT or your name across every page.

How to use Add watermark

  1. Tap Choose a PDF file and pick your document.
  2. Type your watermark text, for example DRAFT or your name.
  3. Set the strength with the slider and decide if the text should slant across the page.
  4. Tap Add watermark, then download the stamped PDF.

About this tool

Add watermark stamps a line of text across every page of a PDF. Use it to mark a document as DRAFT or CONFIDENTIAL, or to put your name on work you share so that others know where it came from.

The text is grey and semi-transparent, so the page stays readable. Use the strength slider to make it lighter or stronger, and choose whether the text runs diagonally across the page or straight along it. The size adjusts to each page automatically.

A watermark is permanent in the new file, so keep your original PDF safe in case you need a clean copy later. The watermark supports English letters, numbers and common symbols.

Your file stays on your device. It is processed inside your browser and is never uploaded to a server.

Questions

Can I use Hindi or another language for the watermark?

Not yet. The watermark supports English letters, numbers and common symbols only.

Can I remove the watermark later?

No. Keep your original file, because the watermark becomes part of the new PDF.

Will the watermark hide my text?

No. It is semi-transparent grey, so the words on the page can still be read.

Can I add an image or logo?

Not yet. This tool adds text watermarks only.
