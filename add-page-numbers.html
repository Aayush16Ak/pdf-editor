Add page numbers

Print a page number on every page of your PDF.

How to use Add page numbers

  1. Tap Choose a PDF file and pick your document.
  2. Choose the position: bottom centre, bottom right, bottom left or top right.
  3. Choose the style, 1, 2, 3 or Page 1 of 10, and set the first page number.
  4. Tap Add page numbers, then download the numbered PDF.

About this tool

Add page numbers puts a number on every page of a PDF. It is useful for assignments, reports, project files and merged documents that need to look organised before you print or submit them.

Pick where the number appears and choose between a plain number, such as 7, or the longer style, such as Page 7 of 20. You can also start counting from a number other than 1, which helps when your PDF is a continuation of another document.

Numbers are added in a small dark grey font and appear upright even on pages that were scanned sideways.

Your file stays on your device. It is processed inside your browser and is never uploaded to a server.

Questions

Can I start from a number other than 1?

Yes. Change the First page number box to any number you like.

Can I skip the cover page?

Not directly. Use Split PDF to separate the cover page, number the remaining pages, then join them again with Merge PDF.

Will the numbers show on rotated pages?

Yes. They are placed so that they appear upright on the page as you see it.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. The numbers are added in your browser.
